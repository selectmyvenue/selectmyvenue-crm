Candidate journey — complete in order

01 · Applicant

Create candidate record, collect CV and contact details.

02 · Screening

Check experience, availability, location and role fit; record notes.

03 · Interview

Discuss duties, work mode/hours, reporting, travel and expectations.

04 · Selected

Confirm job type, pay/commission, payout rules, start date and manager.

05 · Offer sent

Generate correct letter, review, approve, PDF it, send and collect signed copy.

06 · Onboarding

Collect signed agreement, policy/NDA, joining form and necessary documents.

07 · Active

Complete document checks, CRM access, induction and first-week goals.

Document safety: Do not collect Aadhaar/PAN/bank details at application stage. Collect only what is genuinely required, explain why, restrict access and never request OTPs or passwords.

🔒 Data & compliance boundary

This portal currently supports people records, compensation fields, draft letters, document register and onboarding tasks. Leave / attendance workflows, payroll processing, expense claims, statutory registers and appraisals are not implemented. This dashboard is not proof of statutory compliance.
